The Journey locomotion · terrain tiers · day/night · weather · wind · checkpoints

A traveler walks the real Sunfall Meadow: run promotes from walk, scramble hills slow and drain, the 73° cliff triggers the true-climb reach cycle, slopes slide, weather rolls through, and the day turns at 40× speed. Nothing here is scripted footage — it's the engine playing itself.

A Whole Life aging system · height · facial hair · stat curve

Age is tied to time, not distance. Drag through a lifetime — height grows from the feet up, the head ratio evens out, stubble arrives in late Youth, hair and beard grey together, the Elder stoops. The stat curve tells the same story: children recover fast and hit soft; elders trade muscle for casting.

Three Paths classes · auto-aim · accuracy · targeting tags

Thief knives and Wizard bolts auto-acquire targets — skill lives in positioning, and trained Accuracy decides whether shots land (watch the occasional knife sail wide). The Fighter's cleave is melee-only by design: the bat lane shows the sword literally unable to touch the sky.

Seven Worlds 6-layer parallax · authored palettes · biome chaining

Every biome is a complete scene — its own palette, treeline density, terrain profile, weather table, and musical key, authored as one JSON file each. The Mountain Pass splits into two of them: a multi-path fork where your build picks the road.

Adaptive Score generative stems · layered tension/combat/town · crossfades

No audio files exist — every note is synthesized live from each biome's authored key, tempo, and scale via Web Audio. Pick a biome's theme, then fade the tension drone, combat pulse, or town warmth in and out: the same layers gameplay drives automatically.

Carry Your Journey portable save codes · CRC-checked · human-friendly

Any checkpoint save packs into a ~50-character code — bit-packed binary, CRC-16 checked, Base32 without confusable letters. Build a save below and watch it encode live; a single mistyped character is caught before it can ever load.
